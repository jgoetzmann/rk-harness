`rk-work/events.jsonl`. It answers four questions the owner asked: unblock what is blocked, turn
the adaptive and implicit paths on and get them onto both sites, close the open engineering
decisions with recommendations, and file the proposals that follow.

Companion documents: rulings are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` (D13 onward); future work is in
`docs/proposals/`. The side-track design this builds on is `docs/SIDETRACK-AUTOMATION.md`.

## 2. Three locks, and how they compound

**Read sections 2 and 3 as a snapshot taken at 2026-09-08T02:42Z, before any of this landed.**
They describe the faults in the present tense because that is what the plan was written against.
All three are fixed and deployed; section 7 records what was measured afterwards. Where a statement
here says something does not exist, it means it did not exist at 02:42Z on 2026-09-08.

### Lock 1, the Codex gate latched shut

`runner.py:285-316` `_codex_rate_limits()` reads the newest `rate_limits` line out of
`$CODEX_HOME/sessions/rollout-*.jsonl`. Four gates consult it: `runner.py:491` (directive),
`runner.py:669-670` `_codex_capped` (literature), `runner.py:774` (hypothesis), and
`_maybe_interpret`. The comparison is `>=`.

The last Codex call ran at 2026-09-06T10:40:45Z and reported `used_percent 80.0` against
`cap_percent 80.0`, so the gate closed. Only a Codex call writes a rollout file, so the snapshot
that closed the gate is also the last snapshot that will ever be written. The gate cannot reopen
on its own.

The same snapshot carries `window_minutes 10080` and `resets_at 1788748764`, which is
2026-09-07T02:39:24Z. That window reset over a day ago. Nothing reads `resets_at`.

Consequence: 1,108 `llm_skipped` events, and every directive for more than a thousand cycles has
come from `directive.fallback_directive` rather than the model. Three of the four gates log
nothing at all, so most of the suppression is invisible in `events.jsonl` and in the watcher.

`scripts/run.ps1:67` mounts only `auth.json`, so the sessions directory is container-local, and
`scripts/run.ps1:56` runs `docker rm -f rk` before `docker run`. A restart clears the latch as a
side effect.

### Lock 2, the fallback asks for a cell that cannot exist

`directive.py:193` `_emptiest_cell` scans `stages in range(2, 7)` and `bucket in range(8)` and
returns the first absent cell of the phase's target order. `_TARGET_ORDER_BY_PHASE` maps phase 3
to order 4. `grid[4][(2, bucket)]` can never be filled, because an explicit two-stage Runge-Kutta
method cannot exceed order 2. So `_emptiest_cell` returns `(2, 0)` on every call, permanently.

Live confirmation: `directive_fallback` events D-F02304, D-F02305 and D-F02306 all read
`target_order 4, stages [2]`, and `rk-findings/docs/` has no `cell-p4-s2` page and cannot have
one. `search.project_or_lower` then drops the request to the highest order exactly solvable at two
stages, which is a small space the search exhausted long ago. That is the mechanism behind the
skipped-hash ratio above.

The two locks compound. Lock 1 removed the model directives that were steering the search away
from the unfillable cell; lock 2 is what the search does when nothing steers it.

### Lock 3, the side tracks ship disabled

`config.json` `run.sidetrack_every_cycles` is 0 and `runner.py:651-653` guards on `every <= 0`
before the modulo, so the feature is inert. A search over the whole 45 MB `events.jsonl` returns
zero side-track events, `rk-work/sidetrack/` does not exist, and the findings site has no
`sidetrack.html`. The wiring is complete and loaded (`runner.py:33` imports the module,
`runner.py:991` calls `_maybe_sidetrack` as step 5b), and `sidetrack.py` predates the running
process. Only the setting keeps it off.

### The machine, for completeness

Three containers of a supabase stack on this host are in a restart loop, at 2553, 2621 and 2564
restarts over roughly 44 hours. The cause is not load-related and is not this project's: their
database container `supabase_db_admin` exited 127 nine days ago, so `supabase_auth_admin`,
`supabase_realtime_admin` and `supabase_storage_admin` fail DNS resolution for it at boot and are
restarted forever by their restart policy. `supabase_kong_admin` exited 127 at the same time.

That stack cannot work in its current state, so the restart loop is pure background load. Stopping
the three loopers is an owner action, not a step this plan takes. It is also the precondition for
D20: restoring the shipped CPU pause thresholds on a host with a roughly 64 percent idle baseline
would pause the run and, at a resume threshold of 30 percent, would not release it.

## 3. Four claims in the handoff note that the data does not support

Recorded because the plan's sequencing depends on the corrections, not to relitigate anything.

1. **"Overhead per cycle is measurably down, 77 s, five archive passes to one."** Five passes to
   one is correct and is asserted by `test_A80_fold_is_identical_to_a_replay_after_appending`. The
   77 s is not: it is `SIDETRACK-AUTOMATION.md:351`'s side-track catalogue wall clock, which is
   unrelated. The archive change is worth about **118 s per cycle**, from 468.0 s to 349.5 s at
   matched workload.
2. **"Total cycle time went up because contention took back more than the change gave."**
   Contradicted by every window in `events.jsonl`. No period after the change shows cycle time
   rising. The question the note calls unproven is answered, and the answer is that the
   optimization worked.
3. **"Five jobs across 40 points already ran, and three results are in hand."** The run happened,
   on the host, into a scratch `RK_WORK_DIR` on 2026-09-04, and that directory is not in the
   repository. No artifact survives: `rk-work/sidetrack/` does not exist and no ledger exists
   anywhere. Every number in `SIDETRACK-AUTOMATION.md` section 6a is prose-only and cannot be
   published under the house rule that numbers trace to a data file. An independent recomputation
   from the shipped code reproduces the J4 scan and finds two defects in that prose, recorded in
   D24.
4. **"The explicit wall is total on the stiff suite."** Too strong. `rk-work/validation/results.json`
   shows euler, heun2 and midpoint all finishing `robertson_scaled` in Q15 at the 65,536-cycle
   budget, at 4369, 1680 and 1985 steps, with midpoint winning outright. What is true and traceable
   is `verdicts.per_problem.robertson_scaled.finishers_discovered = 0`. The SDIRK2 result is float64
   with a finite-difference Jacobian on a ladder capped at n=256, and `sidetrack.py:577` stamps it
   `"float64 only; no Q15 effects are included"`. It does not show an implicit method finishing in
   Q15, which is the property epoch 1 measured. D24 sets the wording rule.

## 6. Phases

| Phase | Content | Risk | Gate to the next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Measure the baseline (section 1) | none | done |
| P1 | C1-C4, documentation only | none | none |
| P2 | C5-C7, site safety | low | the suite is green |
| P3 | C8-C14, the two code fixes | low | the suite is green, `VERIFIER_HASH` unmoved |
| P4 | C15-C16, enable and restart | medium | see acceptance |
| P5 | C17-C19, the overview | low | both node checks pass |
| P6 | C20 and the proposals | low | - |

**P2 lands before P4, and the ordering is not cosmetic.** `sitegen.check_banned` runs over every
page in `build()` before any file is written, so one banned word anywhere fails the whole site
build. The side-track ledger is append-only, and a failed point records the exception string. An
unlucky exception string would take the findings site down and keep it down. The renderer has to
be safe before a ledger can exist.

**The opening firing happens in the container, after the restart, and not on the host.** A host
firing would write `rk-work/sidetrack/ledger.jsonl` immediately; the container stages that
directory every cycle and `sitegen` publishes `sidetrack.html` as soon as a ledger exists, so the
page would go live before P2. One route, one order.

**One restart, not two.** The harness is a read-only bind mount and `entrypoint.sh:35` execs a
single long-lived process, so C8 through C14 change nothing until the container is recreated. The
same recreate clears the Codex latch, because `scripts/run.ps1:56` does `docker rm -f rk` and the
sessions directory is container-local. Clearing the latch by hand inside the running container
would reopen the gate against the old `directive.py`, which is exactly the combination to avoid:
model directives resuming while the fallback still points at an impossible cell.

## 7. Acceptance

Checked after P4, in this order.

* **A1.** `VERIFIER_HASH` is byte-identical before and after P1 through P5, and the container's
  start-up check passes.
* **A2.** The full suite is green before the restart, and the container's own start-up gate passes
  after it.
* **A3.** Within one cycle of the restart, `events.jsonl` carries a `codex_usage` event with a
  `used_percent` reading taken after 2026-09-07T02:39:24Z, and a directive whose `source` is not
  `fallback`.
* **A4.** No `directive_fallback` event carries a `stages` value below its `target_order`, except
  at order 1.
* **A5.** Within 20 cycles, `sidetrack_started` and `sidetrack_done` events appear, and
  `rk-work/sidetrack/ledger.jsonl` exists.
* **A6.** `sidetrack.html` appears on the findings site, the site build succeeds, and a second
  build over unchanged inputs is byte-identical.
* **A7.** `candidates_processed` `skipped/total` falls below its current 0.7 to 0.9 band, and the
  median accepted per cycle rises above 8. Measured over two hours, not one cycle.
* **A8.** The published coverage fraction changes from 18/160 to 17/136 on both sites, and the
  change is stated in the register, because it is a number a reader may have quoted. The
  denominator is summed per order (40 + 40 + 32 + 24), and the numerator drops by one because
  euler's single-stage cell sits outside the searchable stage range and is now reported beside the
  fraction rather than counted into it.

A7 is the one that can fail without anything being broken: D19 removes the mechanism that made new
elites impossible in the requested cell, but it does not guarantee that the reachable cells still
hold anything. If A7 does not move within two hours of quiet running, that is a result about
epoch-1 saturation and belongs in the saturation record, not a reason to revert.
